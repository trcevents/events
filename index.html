If Yuh Know, Yuh Know

Chicago Vybez brings the whole Caribbean diaspora out to the park for four straight days of Labor Day Weekend — Jamaica, Haiti, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados, Belize, the Bahamas, Cuba, the Dominican Republic, Guyana, Suriname, Antigua & Barbuda, St. Kitts & Nevis, St. Lucia, St. Vincent & the Grenadines, Grenada, Dominica, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. and British Virgin Islands, all flying flags side by side. Presented by Prestige Promotion, it's free admission every day: sound systems, good food, and fresh cuts, all in one spot.

The catch: the location stays secret until you text in. That's the vybez — real ones already know how this works.
