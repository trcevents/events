Production Packet — Jay RebL × TRC Events

Technical Rider & Run of Show

"International Reggae Day: Roots to Modern Sound" — a 2.5-hour acoustic-to-electric park showcase. Draft specs below are a professional starting point, not confirmed equipment — finalize every line with the venue and Jay RebL's sound engineer once a date and site are locked.

01 — Personnel & Roles

On Site

Jay RebL

Lead vocals, acoustic & electric guitar, host/MC for the sound-system transition

Backing musicians (2-3)

Bass, percussion/Nyabinghi drums, second guitar or keys — headcount to be finalized with the band

Sound system selector(s) (1-2)

Foundational riddim juggling for Act II and bed tracks under Act III

FOH sound engineer

One dedicated engineer for the full 2.5-hour set — not shared with another act's changeover

Stage manager / production lead

Runs the show clock, cues transitions between acts, is the single point of contact for the venue

02 — Stage, Space & Power

Site Requirements

Dual-Stage Layout

A park shell or open lawn with room for two zones: a small acoustic circle at ground level (Act I) surrounded by audience seating, leading into the main amplified stage (Acts II-III). A shell or covered stage is preferred but not required if weather contingency (Section 05) is in place.

Power

Two dedicated 20A circuits minimum — one for backline/stage power, one for the FOH sound system — plus generator backup if the site has no house power. Exact draw depends on the final PA size; confirm with whichever sound vendor the venue or NOITP assigns.

04 — Backline

Who Brings What

Jay RebL / band brings

  • Acoustic guitar with pickup, tuner
  • Electric guitar + pedalboard
  • Selector's own controller/mixer and music library
  • Hand percussion (if the percussionist supplies their own)

Needed from venue/promoter

  • Bass amp or DI-friendly bass rig
  • Drum kit or Nyabinghi drum set (if not traveling with the percussionist)
  • 3-4 vocal mic stands, 2 instrument stands
  • Monitor wedges x3-4 (or in-ear pack if the budget supports it)

05 — Hospitality & Logistics

Standard Asks

  • Shaded green room or tent area near the stage for the full call time

  • Bottled water for 6-8 people through the full program

  • Reserved parking for 2 vehicles (band + gear load-in)

  • Load-in window of at least 90 minutes before doors

07 — Weather & Contingency

Outdoor Show, Standard Precautions

Confirm the host park's rain-date or covered-shell policy before the date is set — NOITP sites vary on this. Backline and FOH gear need weather covers on hand regardless of forecast. Reconfirm the site's amplified-sound curfew (city noise ordinances vary by park) before finalizing the Act III end time.
