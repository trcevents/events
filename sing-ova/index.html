The Cover Story Format

Every chapter runs the same eight-week rotation — the format never changes, only the pairings do. Week 8 hands the mic to the crowd.

Week 1

Lovers Rock vs. Slow Jams

Reggae lovers rock paired with R&B slow jams.

Week 2

Riddim Rewind

One riddim traced across eras, artists, and genres.

Week 3

Dancehall x Hip-Hop

Dancehall anthems beside the hip-hop records they influenced.

Week 4

Roots & Culture

Message-driven reggae with a deeper cultural frame.

Week 5

Unexpected Voices

White/Latino paired with dancehall/reggae crossover.

Week 6

Throwback Sunday

60s/70s R&B, reggae, and dancehall side by side.

Week 7

Queens of the Riddim

Women artists across R&B, reggae, and dancehall.

Week 8

Wildcard / Request Sunday

Crowd pairings, resident picks, and a guest selector.
